📢 Official Updates
Company

Anthropic's IPO Is Reportedly Multiple Times Oversubscribed

Bloomberg reports demand for Anthropic's IPO is several times the shares on offer, just a day into the roadshow. The deal is 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78: about 801.4 million new shares plus 23.6 million sold by existing holders, listing on Nasdaq as ANT.

Separately, seven tech companies including Google, Nvidia, Microsoft, Broadcom and TSMC Partners committed $26 billion to a nonvoting Class N private placement. Combined, Anthropic could raise up to $90.4 billion, with a fully diluted valuation near $1.4 trillion at the top of the range. Pricing is expected around Nov. 18.

Policy

Updated Supported Regions Policy Takes Effect

Anthropic's revised Supported Regions Policy kicked in on Nov. 12. It clarifies that using Claude while physically in mainland China, Hong Kong or Macau is restricted, even if the account is registered in a supported region. It covers Claude's apps, the API, and models served through cloud platforms and resellers, plus entities headquartered in unsupported regions.

When an account shows activity from an unsupported region, Anthropic may ask for ID verification, and accounts that fail are banned. Users report sudden KYC prompts and suspensions, including long-time Max subscribers. Anthropic hasn't said how many accounts are affected. Taiwan stays on the supported list.

Infrastructure

S-1 Updates Spell Out the Compute Bill

New filings add about $10 billion in cloud commitments with Microsoft and Google, per The Information. Anthropic's minimum Microsoft commitment rises by up to $4.3 billion, to as much as $49.2 billion through 2033.

Anthropic also disclosed that its $34.9 billion Lambda agreement can be terminated by either side after Lambda missed a facility-lease deadline. Neither party has given notice.

Fable 5.2 Tops the Remote Labor Index

According to a report from The Decoder, Fable 5.2 completed 41.3% of real Upwork projects on Scale Labs' Remote Labor Index, about double Opus 5.5. That's a third-party benchmark, not an Anthropic claim.

Earlier coverage put Claude Fable 5 at roughly 16% on the same test. That's a steep climb, so check the leaderboard methodology before you quote it.

Regulation

New York Opens Frontier AI Registration

New York's RAISE Act registration window is open. Developers with more than $500 million in prior-year revenue and models trained with over 10^26 operations must register with the state's AI office, and TechCrunch reports OpenAI and Anthropic must do so by Jan. 1.

Obligations include 72-hour critical-incident reports, quarterly catastrophic-risk assessments and a compliance disclosure every two years. Penalty amounts aren't set yet.

🧠 Analysis
Take

Wall Street Is Buying Work, Not Software

Goldman Sachs reportedly models Anthropic at about $685 billion in revenue by 2030. The BitDigest newsletter argues the bet is on labor, not seats: roughly 79% of revenue is metered token use. A Remote Labor Index score that doubled in one model generation fits that story.

Two things to watch before pricing. The $26 billion placement comes from Anthropic's own suppliers and partners, which is support but also a little circular. And tightening region rules days before the listing reads like clearing compliance risk while every investor is reading the S-1.

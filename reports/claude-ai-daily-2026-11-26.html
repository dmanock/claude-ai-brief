KPMG Puts Claude Mythos on Cyber Duty

KPMG LLP launched a set of cybersecurity services powered by Claude Mythos, Anthropic's security-focused frontier model. The work covers four areas: scanning codebases for vulnerabilities, mapping identity-based attack paths, tracing incident and breach pathways, and assessing data retention and security risk.

KPMG is part of Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program, the gate for using Mythos on offensive-adjacent security work. No pricing or deal size was disclosed, but a Big Four firm productizing Mythos is a strong signal for the model's enterprise reach.

Yes, You Can Get Banned for Being Cruel to Claude

Infor Brings Claude to the Factory Floor

Infor and Anthropic launched Infor Industry AI for Claude, a plugin and connector built on MCP. Users ask questions in Claude and approve proposed actions, which Infor then executes, with a human approval required before each change.

First roles: production planning, purchasing and customer service. It's available to select Infor Velocity Suite customers who bring their own Claude license and have an admin switch on the connector. A webinar is set for Dec. 3 at 11 a.m. ET.

Claude Ship: The Fine Print

More detail on Monday's Claude Ship launch: Anthropic says merged changes more than doubled from June to September for teams using its review agents, while later reverts fell by more than 40%. Billing is by token use.

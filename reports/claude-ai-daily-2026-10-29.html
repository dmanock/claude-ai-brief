Government

Anthropic Expands GSA Deal With 40% Discount

Anthropic widened its General Services Administration agreement. Federal users get 40% off many services, including Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government and the Claude API. The discount now extends to state, local, tribal and territorial governments too.

About 500 US government organizations already use Claude. Weekly active users of Claude for Government are up roughly eightfold since February, and federal civilian agency usage has grown about 20-fold in a year.

Claude for Legal Gets Artifacts and 60+ Connectors

Anthropic expanded Claude for Legal with a gallery of ready-made artifacts for contract review, citation checks, contract comparison and regulatory monitoring. It also added more than 60 connectors, including Bloomberg Law, Filevine and Legora.

The expansion follows May's launch and includes tools built with Goodwin, Harvey and Foley & Lardner.

Research

Study: Analysts Took the Agent's Lean, Then Skipped the Credit

In an Anthropic study, 50 professional analysts used Claude Code to evaluate a farming cooperative's advisory service. They chose the agent's lean 86% of the time, where chance is about 50%.

Afterward, 77% used the agent's arguments in their own write-ups. Only 15% credited the agent.

🧠 Analysis
Take

The Real Enterprise Risk Is the Argument You Didn't Check

Government discounts and legal connectors are Anthropic widening the funnel into regulated, high-stakes work. Fair enough. But its own study is the sharper story: people took the agent's view, reused its reasoning and rarely said so.

In law and government, the failure mode isn't a wrong answer. It's an unexamined one. Expect audit trails, citation checks and attribution to become selling points, not footnotes.

Anthropic Says It Has Met Its AI-Acceleration Threshold

Gizmodo spotted a quiet but big change between S-1 drafts. The Nov. 2 filing said Anthropic may imminently meet the Responsible Scaling Policy criteria for dramatically accelerated progress attributable to AI. The Nov. 10 amendment says Anthropic has made a determination that it has met that threshold.

The call is based on expected future models, not something already observed, so this isn't a recursive self-improvement event. But the founders' letter says building AI safely may mean modulating the pace of capability gains, and that's now sitting in an investor document two days before pricing.

IPO Countdown: Pricing Wednesday, ANT Trades Thursday

Anthropic prices on Wednesday, Nov. 18, and ANT opens on the Nasdaq Global Select Market Thursday. The range is still $70 to $78 on 825 million shares, a raise of up to $64.4 billion plus a $26 billion concurrent placement, roughly $90 billion combined.

Rate Limit and Usage Docs Get a Cleanup

Anthropic tidied its limits docs over the weekend, per ClaudeNetwork's tracker. The API usage limits outline was condensed, an interactive search helper was pulled from the rate limits page, and the consumer usage-limits article now points to the Team plan page instead of the individual plan page.

No quotas, prices or token allowances changed. Last week's ramp-limit guidance still stands, so keep easing traffic up and honoring retry-after headers.

Claude Code

Claude Code Week in Review: Code Mode, Fast Vertex, Sandbox Change

This week's roundup of agentic CLIs tracks Claude Code from 2.1.321 to 2.1.325: permission and sandbox fixes, fast mode on Vertex, an experimental Code Mode opt-in, and plugin gating via an engines field.

The one to check before you upgrade: sandboxed Bash commands now go through the normal permission flow unless you set sandbox.autoAllowBashIfSandboxed: true. 2.1.325 also patches a security issue in zstd response decoding.

Monday Outage Hits Sonnet 5.5 and Fable 5.2

Elevated errors for Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.2 hit claude.ai, the Claude API, Claude Code and Cowork from 07:49 to 09:04 UTC, about 75 minutes. It was marked resolved, though claude.ai showed a partial outage again around 13:00 UTC.

That makes seven incidents in November, totaling more than 35 hours of disruption across Claude components. Not the reliability story anyone wants in IPO week.

Take

Safety Policy Is Now Investor Disclosure

Anthropic is telling public-market buyers, in writing, that its own safety framework says AI is starting to speed up AI, and that it may slow down because of it. No other lab has put that in a prospectus.

That cuts both ways. It's the most credible safety signal Anthropic has sent. It's also a risk factor: a company that may deliberately pump the brakes is a different stock than one that won't. Watch whether Wednesday's price shrugs it off.

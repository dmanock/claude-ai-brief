Anthropic and Barclays expanded their work together on October 1. More than 16,000 colleagues already use a Claude-powered Colleague Knowledge Assistant, which handles over one million searches to support 20+ million UK retail customers.

Sonnet 5.5 Rounds Out the 5.5 Family

Claude Sonnet 5.5 launched September 28 as a faster, lower-cost sibling to Opus 5.5. It runs 30%+ faster, costs up to 30% less per task, and scores 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0. Per-token pricing holds at $2 input and $10 output per million.

It is the first Sonnet with Opus-level cybersecurity safeguards, and it is on Claude Platform, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure.

Claude Code 2.1.287 Introduces Claude Mods

The October 1 release lets plugins modify deeper Claude Code behavior, and ships a built-in “You should know” mod. MCP servers can now prompt users with URLs for browser-based auth flows under the 2025-11-25 protocol.

Reliability got love too: Remote Control reconnects with a 30-second timeout and retry, large MCP tool results use less memory, and Windows Bash is faster.

Migrating to Sonnet 5.5? Change the Thinking Setting

Moving to claude-sonnet-5-5 means switching the thinking setting from off to between_tools. Old calls that assumed the previous behavior will need a tweak.

Cache reads run $0.20 per million tokens and cache writes $2.50. Zero data retention is available.

Appeals Court Upholds Pentagon’s Supply-Chain-Risk Label

In a 2-1 ruling on September 25, the D.C. Circuit backed the Pentagon’s March designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk. The fight centers on Anthropic refusing to drop limits on lethal autonomous warfare and mass surveillance of Americans.

Judge Henderson dissented, warning the reading could enable coercive demands on future contractors.

📢 Official Updates
Product

Claude Meetings Rolls Out in Beta

Claude Meetings is now rolling out on Team and Enterprise plans in Claude and Claude Desktop. You add Claude to a Google Meet call as a visible participant, and it takes notes, answers questions and builds artifacts like docs, whiteboards and slides while the call is still going.

Notes and transcripts are free during the beta, and artifacts made in meetings are free through Feb. 10, 2027. Orgs on zero data retention, HIPAA-ready setups or customer-managed keys are excluded for now.

Policy

Anthropic's Revised Usage Policy Takes Effect

The Usage Policy Anthropic announced on Oct. 8 is now live as of Nov. 12. The headline addition bars sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward Claude itself. Ordinary frustration with a wrong answer isn't covered.

Enforcement leans mainly on Claude's existing ability to end a conversation. The update also tightens the rules on surveillance and weapons, so teams building in those areas should reread the policy.

Partnership

Infor and Anthropic Bring Claude to the Factory Floor

Infor announced Infor Industry AI for Claude, a plugin and connector built on MCP. Manufacturers can ask questions in Claude and, after approval, take action in Infor: create purchase orders, reallocate stock to an urgent order, shift production lines after unplanned downtime or ship an order.

It's live for select Infor Velocity Suite customers, who need their own Claude license and an admin to switch the connector on. Infor calls Claude its preferred AI assistant. It lands alongside Anthropic's new Claude for Manufacturing plugin.

Login Outage Hits claude.ai and Claude Code

Starting at 09:58 UTC Friday, users couldn't sign in to claude.ai or Claude Code. By 11:05 UTC Anthropic said a fix was deployed and it was monitoring recovery. The incident was renamed to elevated errors on claude.ai, Claude Code and platform.claude.com, and Cowork was added to the list.

The Claude API stayed operational, so production apps on API keys were mostly fine. No root cause has been posted yet. Separately, a Microsoft 365 connector incident on Nov. 12 affected mail, calendar and search.

Legal

Claude Max Usage Lawsuit Moves Forward

A federal magistrate judge in Northern California let key claims proceed in a suit saying Claude Max's '5x or 20x more usage' marketing doesn't hold up against weekly caps. Deceptive-advertising, negligent-misrepresentation and breach-of-contract claims survive. An unfair-competition claim was dismissed.

It's not a finding that the ads were misleading, and no class has been certified. Max costs $100 a month for 5x and $200 for 20x. Expect usage caps to get more scrutiny as more people pay for them.

🧠 Analysis
Take

Claude Is Infrastructure Now

An hour-long login outage would have been a shrug two years ago. In the week of a trillion-dollar-plus IPO, with Claude sitting in Google Meet calls and factory ERPs, it's a real event. Every launch this week assumes Claude is always on.

The usage policy and the Max lawsuit point the same way. Anthropic is being held to the standards of a utility: clear rules, honest limits, reliable uptime. That's the trust test public-market investors will keep running.

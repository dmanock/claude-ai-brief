Anthropic Formally Closes Its $93.5B IPO

Anthropic announced the close of its initial public offering: 1.127 billion Class A shares at $83.00, including full exercise of the underwriters' option to buy 147 million additional shares, plus a concurrent private placement.

Per Defiance's filing summary, that's roughly $93.5 billion in gross proceeds. The prospectus showed $33.2 billion in revenue for the nine months to Sept. 30 against a $104 billion net loss, so expect the compute bill to stay the central question.

Claude Code

Claude Code 2.1.330 Adds Enterprise Admin Console Sign-In

Released Nov. 20, version 2.1.330 lets Claude Code sign in to organizations managed in Anthropic's Enterprise Admin Console. After claude auth login, it applies the org's settings and routes through its cloud provider or gateway.

Also new: a built-in mod called easel that draws charts (Pro, Max and, where available, Team and Enterprise, on macOS and Linux; switch it off in /config), a --start-dir option for self-hosted runners, and CLAUDE_CODE_FULL_SIGN_IN_LINK=1 to print the full sign-in link.

Claude Code

Catching Up: What 2.1.329 Changed

If you skipped a version, 2.1.329 (Nov. 19) lists scheduled tasks, /loop wakeups and forks in /tasks, adds a Server message row in /mcp for remote HTTP MCP refusals, and fixes repeated macOS sign-outs when Claude Code could read the Keychain but not write to it.

It also removed 2.1.326's automatic retry when a request hits the max_tokens limit, so long outputs fail fast instead of silently retrying.

Markets

Four Leveraged Anthropic ETFs Hit the Market

Defiance launched its 2x long ANTC and 2x short ANTQ on Nasdaq this morning, each with a 0.71% expense ratio before swap costs. Tradr's 2x long ANTT and 2x short ANTZ were also slated to start trading on Cboe today.

Tradr's own release warns investors could lose everything if ANT moves more than 50% against a fund in a single day. These are daily-reset trading tools, not buy-and-hold positions, and none is affiliated with Anthropic.

Research

The Riemann Claim Goes Mainstream

Coverage of Levent Alpöge's Claude-assisted Riemann proof spread worldwide over the weekend. Alpöge says he worked with Claude for about six weeks and released the argument as Lean code, which the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative said Saturday they're confident is correct.

Anthropic still hasn't formally commented. The Clay Institute requires publication and two years of broad acceptance before its $1 million prize, so the human-readable write-up is what to watch.

ANT Bounces Premarket After a Brutal Friday

ANT fell 8.5% Friday to close at $88.91, just above its $83 IPO price, the same day the Riemann claim went public. Shares rose in premarket trading Monday as tech stocks rebounded.

The overhang is real: up to 303 million more insider shares become sellable Dec. 15, worth about $26.9 billion at Friday's close, with more steps in January and February.

Nvidia's Reversal Gives AI Stocks a Lifeline

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO Monday, effective immediately, after his Nov. 12 resignation. NVDA jumped about 30% at the open and the Nasdaq surged.

For Anthropic, it's a reminder of how tightly AI stocks trade together. Fortune's Term Sheet framed last week as an IPO pop that turned into a broad tech rout. A friendlier tape helps ANT more than any single Claude launch could this week.

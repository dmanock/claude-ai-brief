Security

Anthropic Launches Its Cyber Mission

Anthropic announced a long-term cyber push with two parts. The Critical Infrastructure Defense Program gives partners frontier Claude models, on-site engineers and threat research to protect power grids, water utilities, factories and transport. The OSS Scanner is a free, opt-in service that scans enrolled open-source projects and returns bug reports with a proof-of-concept exploit and a suggested fix.

Anthropic expects a true-positive rate above 90%, but reports go out without human review, so expect some errors. Eleven founding partners: Accenture, Booz Allen, CrowdStrike, Deloitte, Dragos, Hitachi, Insane Cyber, Nozomi Networks, Palo Alto Networks, PwC and Rockwell Automation.

Usage Policy Bans Sustained Cruelty Toward Claude

The updated policy prohibits sustained, needless abusive behavior toward Claude and election campaigns built on deception, effective Nov 12. Examples include flooding the system with abuse or provoking it after a task ends.

There is no automatic suspension. Claude can end a persistently hostile chat on its own. Constructive criticism, frustration over errors, dark fiction and security testing remain allowed.

Build

Ping Labs Ships a Claude-Built Rust TypeScript Compiler

Theo Browne's ts-rust ports the TypeScript 7 compiler to Rust and passes all 181,711 ported Go compiler tests. Claude Opus wrote a v0 in 10 hours and the final run cost about $24K over two weeks. An earlier Codex attempt reportedly cost over $420K and reached about 84% compatibility.

The README claims 1.61x faster than tsc 7 on average. Known issues include memory growth in long editor sessions and possible stale output in multi-project builds.

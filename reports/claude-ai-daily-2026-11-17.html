📢 Official Updates
Company

Who Controls ANT: Founders Keep 50.1% of the Vote

The prospectus gives all seven co-founders one jointly held Class F share through Founder LLC, which carries 50.1% of the voting power. Public buyers get economics, not control. The founders have also pledged to donate 80% of their personal equity to charity.

Anthropic is issuing 801.3M new Class A shares, with selling holders adding 23.6M, at $70 to $78. Post-IPO, Amazon holds about 14.7% and Alphabet about 13.7%. Neither is selling, and both face six-to-12-month lockups. Pricing is Wednesday, trading Thursday.

Take

You’re Buying the Ride, Not the Wheel

ANT buyers get no real say. Founders hold 50.1% of the vote through one share, and Amazon and Alphabet own over a quarter of the company. For a lab whose S-1 says it may slow down for safety, that structure is the point: it lets the founders hit the brakes without a shareholder revolt.

The catch is the price. A $1.4T valuation assumes the accelerator stays down. Tonight’s Nvidia print and Wednesday’s pricing will show how much the market trusts that bet.

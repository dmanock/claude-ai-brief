Personal Finance Arrives in Claude (Beta)

Anthropic launched Personal Finance in beta on Nov. 5 for Pro, Max and Ultra users in the U.S. Connect bank and credit card accounts through Plaid, then ask plain-language questions like where your money went this month. Claude can flag duplicate charges, unexpected fees and rising bills.

It is read-only: Claude cannot move or invest money. It can see account names, last four digits, balances, credit limits and up to two years of transactions. Anthropic says there are no ads or sponsored picks, and Personal Finance chats are not used for training. Per the coverage, it is not built for investment advice.

Claude Enterprise Adds Security Controls

Anthropic announced more visibility and control for security teams on Nov. 5. The list: a security center (beta), generally available inference hooks, centralized admin management (beta) and new access controls. It also adds EDR sensor support for Cowork in beta.

The EDR piece is the follow-on to yesterday's Cowork monitoring news, now bundled into a wider enterprise security pitch.

Developers

Game-Cloning Rule Draws Developer Pushback

Anthropic added two sentences to Claude Code's prompt on Opus 5.5 to steer it away from recreating someone else's specific game. Anthropic's Addy Osmani said on Oct. 30 that original, genre and own-asset games should keep working.

Developers say they also hit refusals on reverse engineering and software analysis. Some switched to Opus 4.8. Those reports are anecdotes, and Anthropic's confirmation covers only the game rule.

Trust Is the Product Now

Read-only access, no ads, no training on chats, EDR hooks and admin controls. Anthropic's pitch this week is the same in consumer and enterprise: you can let Claude in because you can see and limit what it does.

📢 Official Updates
Company

IPO Week Arrives: Pricing Wednesday, ANT Trades Thursday

Anthropic confirmed Thursday that it will price its IPO on Wednesday, Nov. 18, with shares trading on Nasdaq as ANT from Thursday, Nov. 19. The amended S-1 offers 825 million shares at $70 to $78, an implied valuation of $1.3 trillion to $1.45 trillion.

The filing shows $33.2 billion in revenue for the nine months to Sept. 30, up about 1,206%, against $895.4 billion in total committed spend. A concurrent private placement of roughly $26 billion includes up to $10 billion each from Nvidia and Alphabet.

Models

Claude Mythos Preview Ported Claude to AMD GPUs in Three Days

According to Anthropic's IPO deck, Claude Mythos Preview took three days to port and optimize Claude to run on AMD's MI355 GPUs, with Claude writing or porting 100% of the kernels.

Anthropic says the same job would previously have taken a dozen engineers six to 12 months. It's a strong data point for agentic coding at the systems level, and a signal that Anthropic wants room to move beyond any single chip vendor.

API

API Docs Now Define Ramp Limits

Anthropic's rate limit docs now spell out ramp limits for sudden traffic jumps. Bursty workloads can trigger HTTP 429 errors even when you're under your per-minute caps.

No numeric allowances changed. If you run batch jobs or launch-day spikes, ramp traffic up gradually, respect retry-after headers and test your backoff before you need it.

MCP

Adobe Adds an MCP Server for Photoshop

Adobe has put an MCP server on Photoshop, according to this week's Founder's Wire roundup of TechCrunch coverage. MCP is the open protocol Claude uses for connectors, so MCP clients, Claude included, can plug into it.

It's another big creative-software name adopting the standard Anthropic launched. Details on availability and supported actions are in Adobe's announcement coverage.

Legal

Nonprofits Sue to Halt Frontier AI Buildout

Four nonprofits, Public Citizen, Irreplaceable Education Fund, Heat Initiative and Alliance for Secure AI, sued OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and SpaceX's AI unit in San Francisco. They ask a court to bar further frontier model development, Claude included.

The claims rest on California's Unfair Competition Law and public nuisance. No injunction has been issued, and Anthropic had not responded publicly at the time of reporting.

🧠 Analysis
Markets

Investors Flock to the IPO but Question How Long the Lead Lasts

Reuters reports the book was about 2x covered by the end of Tuesday and about six times oversubscribed by Thursday. Banks' models put 2028 revenue at $270 billion to $280 billion, up from roughly $52 billion to $53 billion in 2026 and $135 billion to $137 billion in 2027.

Not everyone is buying the curve. One investor not participating pegs 2028 revenue about 30% below the sell-side view, citing questions about how durable Anthropic's lead is. Anthropic declined to comment.

Take

The Real Moat Might Be Portability

The AMD port is the sleeper story of the IPO. If Claude can retarget itself to new silicon in days, Nvidia lock-in weakens and that $895 billion compute bill gets more negotiable. That matters more than any single quarter of revenue growth.

Wall Street is pricing the revenue curve. The skeptics are asking whether the lead lasts. A company that can move its models to whatever chips are cheapest has a better answer to that question than most.

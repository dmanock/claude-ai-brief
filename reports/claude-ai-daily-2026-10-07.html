Startups

Claude for Startups Gets a Big Expansion

Anthropic announced on October 6 that eligible startups get one free year of Claude Team with up to five premium seats, plus $1,000 in API credits. The program also adds access to Claude Marketplace for building plug-ins and virtual office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team.

To qualify, a company must have been founded within the last five years or raised funding within the last two. It was unveiled during SF Tech Week.

Safety

Anthropic Staff Alerted Police to a Flagged Chat

Futurism reports a Florida woman was arrested on a felony charge after telling Claude she planned to shoot up a nearby sheriff's office. Anthropic's monitoring flagged the conversation, employees judged the threat credible, and they notified local law enforcement.

The sheriff said she treated Claude like a diary, apparently unaware flagged messages can be reviewed. It reopens the debate over user privacy versus AI company responsibility.

Training

Frontier Academy Lists Its First Partners

The $100M Claude Frontier Academy launched October 2 to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers by the end of 2027. First cohorts include Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk.

Engineers do multi-day in-person training, then a 12-week residency leading a real Claude project. First badges are expected in early 2027.

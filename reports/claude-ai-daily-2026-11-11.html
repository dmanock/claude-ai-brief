Product

Claude Moves Into the Office: Meetings, Sheets and Whiteboards

At a San Francisco event on Nov. 10, Anthropic unveiled a batch of workplace tools. Claude Meetings joins Google Meet calls as a visible participant that can draft plans, build whiteboard timelines and pull data from connected apps while people talk. It is in beta for Team and Enterprise plans.

Claude Sheets (tabs, formulas, dropdowns, Excel and CSV export) and Claude Whiteboards (convert a board into a doc or deck) are also in beta. Anthropic's own product lead called Sheets “fairly basic” and not built for heavy financial modeling.

Company

Anthropic Launches Its IPO Roadshow

Anthropic, PBC formally kicked off the roadshow for its IPO. The deal is 825 million Class A shares at an expected $70 to $78 each, listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker “ANT.” Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are lead bookrunners.

At the top of the range that is roughly $64.4 billion raised. Fortune and others put the implied valuation near $1.4 trillion. Underwriters have an option on another 123.75 million shares.

Education

Claude for Teachers Gets Enforceable Privacy Terms

Anthropic adopted contractually enforceable safety and data-privacy standards for school districts using Claude for Teachers. Student and educator data cannot be used to train models, data collection must be minimized, and Anthropic takes on a permanent duty to flag and fix breaches, even after a contract ends.

The terms come from the AFT-backed National Academy for AI Instruction in NYC. The UFT said it was “heartened” but wants more regulation.

Claude Code

Claude Code v2.1.321 Makes Fable 5.2 the Default

The latest Claude Code release switches the default Fable model to claude-fable-5-2 at $8/$40 per million tokens with 1M context. Saved Fable 5.1 picks migrate automatically, and /model claude-fable-5-1 rolls you back.

It also ships security fixes: an MCP OAuth redirect that could leak refresh tokens to another host, read-only Bash commands running outside the sandbox without a prompt, and deny rules not applying to cp/mv into directories. Update now.

Integrations

Claude Data and Interactive Connectors Roll Out

Claude Data, the renamed Claude Dashboards, is now available on paid plans and can turn data into dashboards, data stories or narrated videos. Interactive Connectors let full-app plugins from Adobe, Atlassian and HubSpot run inside Claude.

Anthropic says Claude now reaches more than 9,000 connectors, including Salesforce and Workday. Sources differ slightly on rollout status, so check your plan.

Safety

Meetings Ships With Guardrails for Outside Guests

Claude won't join calls with external guests without admin approval, and it leaves automatically if one joins. It also posts a chat notice when it's in the room.

Anthropic says Claude has sat in on nearly all of its own internal meetings for about two months. That's a strong dogfooding signal, and a sign of where workplace AI is heading.

🧠 Analysis
Take

Anthropic Wants to Be Your Whole Office Before It Rings the Bell

Two days, two launches: cheaper frontier models on Monday, a productivity suite on Tuesday, and a roadshow the same afternoon. The timing is not subtle. Anthropic is showing investors it can sell seats, not just tokens.

The real test is whether teams actually swap out Excel and Meet notes for Claude. Sheets is self-described as basic. Meetings is the sleeper: an AI in every call is a big behavior change, and Anthropic is already living it.
